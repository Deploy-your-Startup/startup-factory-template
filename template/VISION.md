# VISION — Build your idea. Find out what works.

Deploy Your Startup exists to help people turn ideas into working products,
ship features and validate them with real users. We want to make that practical
for anyone with an idea, including developers, students and technical enthusiasts
who want to spend their energy building and learning from feedback.

Projects should start on a working foundation so their owners can focus on the
questions that matter: What do people need? Does this feature help? Will they
use it? What should we try next?

## We take care of the harness

The harness brings together project templates, agent instructions, predictable
service commands, automated checks, deployment and ongoing operations. Useful
defaults resolve recurring setup decisions and make the next feature easier to
build, ship and verify. Owners retain control of their code and infrastructure
and can change those defaults when their product needs it.

Technical decisions should earn their place by helping a product reach users,
improving reliability or preserving owner control. Avoid making people revisit
stack debates and infrastructure choices before they can test an idea. Judge
the foundation by how well it supports building and validation.

## Every startup improves the foundation

Each application keeps its own features and customizations. Lessons that apply
across projects can improve the shared harness without sharing private product
data or an operator's personal portfolio.

Our vision is a harness that gets better step by step through community
contributions: a fix, a clearer instruction, a stronger check or a simpler
deployment benefits the next person and the next startup. Improvements should
remove repeated work and keep existing applications easy to maintain.

## Share the knowledge, keep the portfolio yours

The startup factory makes practical knowledge available to everyone: how to
start, build, test, deploy and operate ideas with coding agents. Share useful
defaults, explanations and lessons so others can learn, adapt them and contribute
improvements back. Community contributions are part of the workflow, not an
optional afterthought.

The factory is a versioned Copier template. Each owner gets an independent
workspace and can review and apply shared improvements through template updates.
The tooling repositories remain in the registry so everyone can inspect their
implementation, propose changes and contribute fixes, tests and documentation.
Contributors use their own forks and pull requests; cloning a repository does
not grant upstream write access.

Distribute reusable knowledge, never personal startup lists, domains, production
contexts, tasks, account identifiers, credentials or private machine settings.
New factories start with an empty startup registry. The owner's portfolio and
Sprowi configuration belong to their instance and must survive template updates.
Personal experience should become a general lesson or a neutral example before
it enters the shared template.

## Apply this vision

- Lead product communication with building, shipping and learning from users.
- Use examples and simple visuals to show what the foundation enables.
- Explain technology when it helps someone make a meaningful decision.
- Prefer improvements that shorten the path from an idea to real feedback.
- Keep ownership, inspectability and customization intact as the harness grows.

This document describes our direction. The [live website](https://deploy-your-startup.com)
and [public onboarding](https://deploy-your-startup.com/get-started) describe
what users can access today; vision statements do not establish current support
or guarantee product success.
